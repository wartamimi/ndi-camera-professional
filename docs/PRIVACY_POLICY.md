# Privacy Policy – NDI Camera Professional

Last updated: 2 November 2026 · Developer: Munawar Tamimi

**What the app does.** The app captures video from the camera and audio from the microphone. It sends them as an NDI® stream to receivers on your local network, but only while you have started a stream.

**Data we collect.** None. The app has no account, analytics, advertising SDK or tracking. Video and audio are not stored or uploaded to any server we operate. They go directly from your device to the NDI receivers on your network.

**Data stored on your device.** Your settings (source name, camera and audio preferences) and your current plan are kept in the app's private storage. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

**Purchases.** Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Google Play. Google's privacy policy applies to that processing. The app receives only purchase status and product IDs from Google Play.

**Permissions.**
- Camera and microphone: to send video and audio.
- Notifications: to show the stream status and a Stop button.
- Network and Wi-Fi multicast: so NDI receivers can discover the stream.

**Children.** The app is not directed at children under 13.

**Contact.** For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Google Play store listing.
